Five ways to sound less like a machine.

Each rule fires independently, at a probability you set, and changes at most one thing per message.

01

Abbreviation R-01

Swaps one common word for the short form people actually type — "vous" becomes "vs".

02

Accent drop R-02

Drops one accent from the message — the kind of slip a fast typist barely notices.

03

Letter omission R-03

Removes one interior letter from a longer word. Mutually exclusive with fusion, below.

04

Word fusion R-04

Merges two adjacent words into one, the way a message sent in a hurry often reads.

05

Punctuation R-05

Drops one comma, semicolon, colon or exclamation mark. A question mark is never touched.
